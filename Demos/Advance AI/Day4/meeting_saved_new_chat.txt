	Replying to "If I were to create a voice agent, that uses strea...":
	For a voice AI agent, governance should apply at every stage of the pipeline.
	
	Input: Transcribe audio, detect/redact PII, and filter unsafe or malicious content before storing or processing.
	
	Processing: Apply guardrails, prompt-injection protection, authorization checks, and maintain audit logs.
	
	Output: Moderate the generated response, avoid leaking sensitive data, and log only sanitized transcripts. Store recordings/transcripts only with proper retention and consent policies.
